# QA checklist
1. Claims and sources are correct; unknown figures explicitly marked as illustrative.
2. Every slide has a clear headline and purposeful content.
3. Slides are readable at presentation size; no overflowing content.
4. All content is inside slide boundaries; alignment and spacing consistent.
5. Charts have truthful values and labels.
6. Colors are consistent and high-contrast.
7. No duplicated or placeholder text.
8. PPTX opens successfully and remains editable.
9. If rendering tools exist, visually review every slide and fix issues. Automated structural validation is not equivalent to visual QA.

## Cross-reference: color system
Follow `color-psychology.md` (Johannes Itten, *The Art of Color*): choose a named contrast strategy, keep semantic color roles stable, and verify contrast. Do not infer unsupported psychological effects from hue.
