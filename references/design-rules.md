# Adaptive design rules
- academic: navy/teal, calm whitespace, clear explanatory structure.
- business: ink/blue, concise executive headlines, metric cards and comparisons.
- technical: charcoal/cyan, workflows, architecture and precise labels.
- creative: plum/coral, strong hierarchy and expressive but readable contrast.
Use 16:9 widescreen. Maintain consistent margins. Title 30–36 pt, body around 20 pt. Use no more than two typefaces. Vary layout intentionally; don't repeat a bullets slide three times consecutively. Prefer native shapes and editable charts. Avoid decorative icons without meaning. Never pretend generated charts contain real observations when values are examples.

## Cross-reference: color system
Follow `color-psychology.md` (Johannes Itten, *The Art of Color*): choose a named contrast strategy, keep semantic color roles stable, and verify contrast. Do not infer unsupported psychological effects from hue.
